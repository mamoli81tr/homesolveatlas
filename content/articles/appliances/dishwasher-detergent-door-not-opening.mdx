Since the dispenser mechanism is a simple spring-and-latch design, most causes are things you can check and often fix without any parts — start with what's easiest to rule out.

| What you notice | Most likely cause |
|---|---|
| Door doesn't move at all | Blocked by a dish, or broken spring |
| Door opens partway, not fully | Sticky residue slowing the latch |
| Door works fine by hand when empty | Something in the load is blocking it |
| Door feels stiff or resistant to open manually | Dried detergent buildup on the latch |
