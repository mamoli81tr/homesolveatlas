Since the top rack cleans normally, the water supply and pump are working — this narrows the problem to something specific to the bottom of the machine, and the filter sitting directly under that rack is the place to look first.

| What you notice | Most likely cause |
|---|---|
| Filter looks visibly dirty or full | Clogged filter |
| Lower arm looks like it isn't moving | Blocked or clogged lower spray arm |
| Problem started after loading a large flat item | Item blocking the arm's rotation |
| Problem persists after cleaning the filter and arm | Water flow issue specific to the lower level |
