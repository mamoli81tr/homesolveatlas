Where the residue shows up on the garment is a genuinely useful clue. Streaks concentrated in folds, cuffs, and pocket edges — the spots where fabric bunches against itself — point toward overloading or restricted rinse flow specifically, since those are exactly the places that get the least water movement in a packed drum. A stiff or waxy feel spread fairly evenly across the whole fabric, not concentrated in folds, points more toward detergent dose or water hardness overall, since those affect the entire garment surface roughly equally rather than just the hard-to-rinse spots.
