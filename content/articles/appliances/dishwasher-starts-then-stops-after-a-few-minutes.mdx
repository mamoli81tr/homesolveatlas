quickAnswer: "A dishwasher that stops before finishing — whether that's within the first few minutes or well into the cycle — with power and lights still on almost always comes down to one of three things: the door latch isn't staying securely closed, a thermal safety switch has tripped, or the control board has hit a fault it can't resolve. Timing (early versus later) is a useful clue but doesn't change the short list of likely causes."
symptoms:
  - "The cycle begins normally — fill, and sometimes wash — then stops on its own"
  - "Lights, display, or a soft hum may remain even though the cycle isn't progressing"
  - "The stop may happen consistently at roughly the same point each time, or vary"
  - "No obvious error code, or a code that isn't explained in your manual"
causes:
  - title: "Door latch not staying fully engaged"
    description: "If the latch doesn't hold securely — from wear, a small obstruction, or vibration gradually loosening it — many machines will pause or stop as a safety response, regardless of how far into the cycle this happens."
  - title: "Thermal safety switch tripped"
    description: "If the machine detects the water or a component running hotter than expected, a safety switch can cut the cycle short to prevent damage — more likely to happen once the heating element has been running for a while, but not exclusively."
  - title: "Control board fault or glitch"
    description: "A control board that loses track of the cycle sequence can stop output partway through, sometimes consistently at a similar stage, sometimes unpredictably."
  - title: "Power supply issue"
    description: "A loose plug, a circuit shared with something that causes brief voltage drops, or a partially tripped breaker can all interrupt a cycle without fully cutting power to the display."
safeChecks:
  - "Open and firmly reclose the door, checking that the latch clicks securely and the door doesn't feel loose."
  - "Note whether the stop happens at roughly the same point every time (suggesting a specific stage-related trigger) or varies."
  - "Check your breaker panel for a partially tripped breaker on the dishwasher's circuit."
  - "Check the plug connection if accessible, confirming it's fully seated."
steps:
  - title: "Check the door latch"
    description: "Confirm the door closes and latches firmly with no wobble, since this is the simplest cause and needs no disassembly to rule out."
  - title: "Power-cycle the machine"
    description: "Switch off at the breaker for a full minute, then restore power and try a normal cycle, watching to see if the stop recurs at the same point."
  - title: "Note the exact timing and any display message"
    description: "Whether it stops at the same stage every time, and any code or light pattern shown, are both valuable details for narrowing down the cause or explaining it to a technician."
  - title: "Try a cooler or shorter cycle setting temporarily"
    description: "If you suspect a thermal trip, testing with a cooler cycle setting can help confirm whether heat buildup is a contributing factor while you arrange service."
dontDo:
  - "Don't force the door latch shut with extra pressure if it feels loose rather than clicking firmly — this can bend the latch further."
  - "Don't keep restarting the same cycle repeatedly without checking the door and breaker first."
  - "Don't attempt to open or test the control board yourself."

Timing alone (a few minutes in versus deep into the cycle) doesn't reliably sort into different causes once you actually check the machine — the same short list of culprits applies either way, so this guide covers both patterns together rather than splitting them into two near-identical pages.

| What you notice | Most likely cause |
|---|---|
| Stops very early, before or right as wash should start | Door latch not engaging fully |
| Stops after the machine has been running a while | Thermal safety trip |
| Stops at a random, inconsistent point each time | Control board fault |
| Display/lights flicker or reset when it stops | Power supply issue |
