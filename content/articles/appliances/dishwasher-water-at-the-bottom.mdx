quickAnswer: "A shallow puddle around the drain area is normal — it keeps the pump seal wet. Standing water that covers the bottom rack floor almost always means the filter, drain hose, or air gap is clogged."
symptoms:
  - "Water visibly pooling across the entire dishwasher floor after a cycle"
  - "Dishes come out with a film or haven't been properly cleaned"
  - "A musty smell coming from the machine"
  - "Gurgling sounds from the kitchen sink during the dishwasher's drain stage"
causes:
  - title: "Clogged bottom filter"
    description: "Food particles collect in the removable filter assembly at the bottom of the tub, restricting how much water the pump can clear."
  - title: "Kinked or clogged drain hose"
    description: "The corrugated hose running to the garbage disposal or drain line can trap food debris or get pinched behind the cabinet."
  - title: "Blocked air gap or disposal knockout plug"
    description: "If connected through an air gap or garbage disposal, a missed knockout plug or clogged air gap can prevent proper drainage."
  - title: "Worn drain valve or failed pump"
    description: "Over time the drain valve or pump motor can wear out and stop clearing water efficiently even when the path is clear."
safeChecks:
  - "Remove the bottom rack and check the filter assembly for trapped food debris."
  - "Run the garbage disposal (if connected) to clear any blockage in the shared drain line."
  - "Look for a kink in the drain hose where it connects behind the dishwasher or under the sink."
  - "Check that the air gap on the counter (the small cylindrical fitting near the faucet, if present) isn't clogged."
steps:
  - title: "Remove standing water"
    description: "Scoop or sponge out any standing water before working on the filter, so you can see what you're doing."
  - title: "Clean the filter assembly"
    description: "Twist and lift out the cylindrical filter (check your manual for the release mechanism), rinse it under running water, and clear any trapped debris."
  - title: "Check and clear the drain hose"
    description: "With the dishwasher pulled out slightly, inspect the hose for kinks and straighten it. If accessible, disconnect and flush it with water over a sink."
  - title: "Clear the air gap or run the disposal"
    description: "Remove the air gap cover and clear debris, or run the garbage disposal for 10–15 seconds to clear the shared line."
  - title: "Run a rinse cycle to confirm"
    description: "Reassemble everything and run a short rinse-only cycle to check that water clears completely."
dontDo:
  - "Don't run a new wash cycle with standing water already in the tub — it can overflow onto the kitchen floor."
  - "Don't pour bleach or drain cleaner into the tub to try to clear it — these chemicals aren't designed for dishwasher components."
  - "Don't ignore a persistently musty smell — it's often linked to the same debris causing the drainage issue."
whenToCallPro:
  - "Water remains after cleaning the filter, hose, and air gap — this suggests a failed drain pump or valve."
  - "The dishwasher makes a loud grinding noise during the drain stage."
  - "You notice water leaking onto the kitchen floor in addition to pooling inside."
  - "The unit is more than 10 years old and this is a recurring issue — replacement may be more cost-effective than repeated repairs."
faqs:
  - q: "Is a small amount of water at the bottom of a dishwasher normal?"
    a: "Yes — a shallow layer around the drain sump keeps the pump seal from drying out. Problems start when water covers the whole tub floor or doesn't recede between cycles."
  - q: "Can I use my dishwasher while there's water at the bottom?"
    a: "It's best to clear it first. Running a cycle with existing standing water increases the risk of overflow, especially if the blockage is still in place."
  - q: "How often should I clean the dishwasher filter?"
    a: "Once a month for average use is a good baseline, more often if you often skip scraping plates before loading."
