Timing the pattern is more useful than just listening for a click. A normal compressor cycle clicks *once* as it starts, and again, once, several minutes to hours later as it stops — long, quiet gaps in between are expected. A relay or compressor problem sounds rhythmic instead: a click every few seconds, repeating in a steady loop, with the compressor never actually settling into its usual hum. If you can count roughly how many seconds pass between clicks and whether that gap stays consistent, you'll know within a couple of minutes which pattern you're dealing with — no need to wait and see if the fridge eventually stops cooling to find out.
