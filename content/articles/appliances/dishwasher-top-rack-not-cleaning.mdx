quickAnswer: "If the bottom rack comes out clean but the top rack doesn't, the pump and water supply are clearly working — the problem is specific to the upper spray arm not getting enough water or rotation. A clogged or blocked upper arm, a loose water-feed connection, or overloading the lower rack are the most common causes."
symptoms:
  - "Bottom rack dishes are consistently clean while top rack items are not"
  - "A film or spotting specifically on glasses and cups stored on the top rack"
  - "The upper spray arm looks like it isn't spinning or is spinning weakly"
  - "The problem is consistent across multiple loads, not a one-off"
causes:
  - title: "Upper spray arm blocked or clogged"
    description: "The same causes covered in our spray arm guide — food debris in the nozzle holes or a tall item on the bottom rack poking up and blocking the upper arm's rotation."
  - title: "Lower rack overloaded, restricting water flow upward"
    description: "A tightly packed bottom rack can block water from reaching the upper spray arm with enough force, even if the arm itself is completely clear."
  - title: "Loose connection where the upper arm's water feed meets the tub wall"
    description: "On many machines, water reaches the top rack through a connector on the tub's back wall — if this has loosened or has a worn seal, less water (or none) reaches the top."
  - title: "Water inlet valve or pump losing pressure under full load"
    description: "Less commonly, a partially restricted inlet or a pump that's lost some strength may deliver just enough pressure for the closer bottom rack but not enough to reach the top effectively."

  - "Look at the connector where the upper rack's water supply tube meets the tub, checking it's fully seated."

faqs:

Because the bottom rack cleans normally, you can rule out the water inlet and pump as the primary suspects right away — this is a top-rack-specific problem, which narrows things down considerably.

| What you notice | Most likely cause |
|---|---|
| Upper arm looks like it isn't moving at all | Blocked or clogged upper spray arm |
| Upper arm spins but weakly | Lower rack overloaded, restricting flow |
| Problem started after loading a very full lower rack | Water flow blocked upward |
| Problem is constant regardless of load size | Loose or damaged upper water feed connector |
