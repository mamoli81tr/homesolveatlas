A spray arm that isn't spinning is a mechanical, not electrical, symptom — the pump is still pushing water, it's just not reaching where it should. That's why this is one of the few dishwasher problems worth checking yourself before assuming anything bigger is wrong.

| What you notice | Most likely cause |
|---|---|
| Only top-rack dishes are dirty | Upper spray arm blocked or clogged |
| Only bottom-rack dishes are dirty | Lower spray arm blocked or clogged |
| Arm rattles instead of spinning | Item blocking rotation path |
| Arm looks fine but spins poorly by hand | Clogged nozzle holes or worn bearing |
