Where the smell seems strongest is a useful clue before you start pulling the machine apart. A smell that's noticeably worse right at the filter, or that gets stronger as you lean in toward the tub floor, points toward trapped food debris — the most common cause and the easiest to fix. A smell that seems to hang around the door itself, especially right when you first open it, more often traces back to the gasket rather than anything inside the tub. And a smell that's present even after the dishwasher has sat unused for a day or two — not just right after a cycle — suggests standing water gone stagnant rather than fresh food debris, which points you toward checking drainage rather than re-cleaning a filter that may already be clean.
