quickAnswer: "A grinding sound during a cycle almost always means a hard object — a fruit pit, bone fragment, piece of broken glass, or a stray bit of hardware — is caught in the chopper blade or pump area that breaks down food waste before draining. It's usually accessible from the filter area for a quick check, but the object itself can be sharp."
symptoms:
  - "A distinct grinding, crunching, or rattling sound during the cycle, often during drain"
  - "The sound may be intermittent rather than constant"
  - "You may recall a recent load with a bone, pit, or fragile item"
  - "Draining may seem slower or less effective alongside the noise"
causes:
  - title: "Hard object caught in the chopper blade"
    description: "Many dishwashers have a small chopper or grinder that breaks down food waste before it drains — bones, fruit pits, and similarly hard items are common causes of a jam here."
  - title: "Broken glass or ceramic fragment"
    description: "A chipped or broken glass or dish can leave sharp fragments that reach the chopper or pump area and cause a grinding sound as they're struck repeatedly."
  - title: "Foreign hardware or utensil fragment"
    description: "A small piece of cutlery, a screw, or other hardware that shouldn't be in the dishwasher at all can cause the same symptom."
  - title: "Worn pump or motor bearing"
    description: "Less common than a jam, but a worn bearing in the wash or drain pump motor can also produce a grinding sound, independent of any trapped object."

Because a chopper blade or pump jam is by far the most common cause, and it's often reachable from the same filter access point used for routine cleaning, this is worth a careful look before assuming a bigger mechanical problem.

| What you notice | Most likely cause |
|---|---|
| Sound is loudest during drain | Object caught in the chopper blade |
| You recall a bone, pit, or chipped dish recently | Hard fragment lodged in the sump |
| Sound is constant, not tied to a specific stage | Possible worn pump or motor bearing |
| Draining also seems slower than usual | Debris also partially blocking flow |
