The hum itself is useful diagnostic information: it means the pump motor is receiving power and attempting to run, which rules out a simple electrical or door-switch problem and points specifically at something mechanically blocking the impeller.

| What you notice | Most likely cause |
|---|---|
| Hum starts immediately and is steady | Impeller jammed by debris |
| Hum sounds strained or labored | Seized bearing or heavy jam |
| No hum at all, total silence | Different fault — see our fills-but-doesn't-wash guide |
| You recall washing a broken or fragile item recently | Broken fragment lodged in the impeller |
