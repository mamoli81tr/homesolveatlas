One test helps localize a stubborn blockage faster than checking every section in turn. With the vacuum running, hold your hand flat over the hose opening (away from the floor head) and listen to the motor. A noticeable change in pitch as you cover and uncover the opening means the motor and main airflow path are working normally — which shifts suspicion toward the floor head, brush roll, or attachment specifically. If covering the hose makes little difference to the sound at all, the restriction is more likely upstream, in the filter or bin, since the motor is already straining against reduced airflow before it even reaches the hose.
