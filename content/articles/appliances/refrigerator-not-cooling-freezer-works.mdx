causes:
  - title: "Frosted-over evaporator coil"
    description: "On many LG models the evaporator coil sits behind a rear panel in the freezer. If the automatic defrost cycle fails, ice builds up and blocks airflow to the fridge."
  - title: "Stuck or broken air damper"
    description: "A motorized damper controls how much cold air moves from the freezer into the fridge. If it's stuck shut or its motor has failed, the fridge gets little to no cold air."
  - title: "Failed evaporator fan"
    description: "This small fan pushes cold air from the freezer evaporator into the fridge compartment. If it stops spinning, the freezer stays cold locally but no air reaches the fridge."
  - title: "Blocked vents inside the fridge"
    description: "Food packages pressed directly against the rear vents can physically block airflow even when everything else works correctly."

  - "Listen near the back wall of the freezer for a faint fan whirring sound — silence can indicate a stalled fan."
  - "Check whether the freezer back panel has visible frost or ice buildup (only if your model allows easy panel access)."
  - "Confirm the door seals on both compartments close fully and aren't cracked or torn."
steps:
  - title: "Clear internal airflow blockages"

This asymmetric cooling pattern — cold freezer, warm fridge — is one of the most common LG refrigerator complaints, and it's mechanical rather than electronic in most cases, which is good news: it's often fixable without replacing the whole cooling system.
