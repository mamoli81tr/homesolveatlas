quickAnswer: "A buzzing sound that continues while the machine still washes and drains normally is usually electrical or vibration-related rather than a jam — most often the drain valve solenoid engaging, a loose panel or bracket vibrating, or the motor working slightly harder than normal. If the machine also fails to wash or drain properly, treat it as a more significant fault rather than just noise."
symptoms:
  - "A steady buzzing or electrical-sounding hum during part of the cycle"
  - "The cycle still completes and dishes still come out reasonably clean"
  - "The sound may be louder at specific stages, like drain or fill"
  - "No grinding, crunching, or straining quality to the sound — more of a smooth buzz"
causes:
  - title: "Drain valve solenoid engaging"
    description: "Some machines use an electrically operated valve to control draining, which can produce a brief buzzing sound as it engages — often completely normal if brief and tied to the drain stage specifically."
  - title: "Loose panel or bracket vibrating"
    description: "An access panel, the kickplate, or an internal bracket that's slightly loose can buzz or rattle in sympathy with normal motor vibration, without indicating any fault with the wash system itself."
  - title: "Motor working slightly harder than normal"
    description: "A motor nearing the point of needing service can sometimes produce a more noticeable hum or buzz under load, even while still completing its job for now."
  - title: "A loose or vibrating utensil or item in the load"
    description: "It's worth ruling out first — a lightweight item vibrating against the rack or tub wall can sound surprisingly like an internal electrical buzz."
safeChecks:
  - "Check the load itself first for anything that could be vibrating against the rack or tub wall."
  - "Note exactly which stage of the cycle the buzzing happens during, especially whether it's tied specifically to drain."
  - "Check accessible panels (like the kickplate) for looseness, tightening if needed once the machine is unplugged."
  - "Confirm the machine is still washing and draining effectively despite the noise, which affects how urgently this needs attention."
steps:
  - title: "Check the load for a vibrating item"
    description: "Open the machine partway through (if safe to pause) or after the cycle and check whether anything in the racks could be the source."
  - title: "Note the timing within the cycle"
    description: "A buzz specifically during drain is more likely the drain valve; a buzz throughout is more likely a panel or motor-related sound."
  - title: "Tighten accessible panels"
    description: "With the machine unplugged, check and gently tighten the kickplate and any accessible access panels that might be loose."
  - title: "Monitor wash and drain performance"
    description: "If cleaning and draining remain normal, this is likely a lower-priority noise issue; if either starts to suffer, treat it as a developing fault worth addressing sooner."

Because the machine keeps working normally, this is generally a lower-urgency symptom than grinding — but it's still worth a quick check to rule out an easy cause like a loose panel or something rattling in the load.

| What you notice | Most likely cause |
|---|---|
| Buzz only during the drain stage | Drain valve solenoid (often normal) |
| Buzz throughout the whole cycle | Loose panel or bracket |
| Wash and drain performance still normal | Lower-urgency, likely vibration-related |
| Cleaning or draining also getting worse | Treat as a developing fault, not just noise |
