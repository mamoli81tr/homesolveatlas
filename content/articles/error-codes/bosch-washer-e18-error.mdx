causes:
  - title: "Clogged pump filter"

  - title: "Kinked drain hose"
    description: "A bent or pinched drain hose, often from the machine being pushed back against a wall without checking hose routing, restricts water flow."
  - title: "Blocked household drain connection"
    description: "If the filter and hose are clear, the blockage may be further downstream in the standpipe or drain line the machine connects to."
  - title: "Failing drain pump"
    description: "If nothing upstream is blocked, the pump motor itself may be wearing out and unable to move water efficiently."
