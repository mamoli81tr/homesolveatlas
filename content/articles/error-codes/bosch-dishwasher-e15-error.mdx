quickAnswer: "E15 means Bosch's Aquastop float switch has detected water pooling in the base pan, so the dishwasher locks out further water intake as a flood-prevention measure. The fix is finding and clearing the source of that water."
symptoms:
  - "'E15' displayed and the dishwasher stops filling or running"
  - "A small amount of water visible in the base of the unit when the kick plate is removed"
  - "The dishwasher ran a normal cycle previously but now won't start"
  - "Error persists across multiple attempts to restart"
causes:
  - title: "Residual water from a previous overflow or leak"
    description: "Even a small, already-stopped leak can leave enough water in the base pan to trigger the float switch."
  - title: "A cracked or loose hose connection"
    description: "The fill or drain hose fittings inside the unit can loosen over time or with vibration, dripping slowly into the base pan."
  - title: "Foam from too much or the wrong detergent"
    description: "Excess suds can escape the tub and drip down into the base, tripping the same sensor as an actual leak."
  - title: "Condensation buildup"
    description: "In rare cases, unusually heavy condensation inside the unit can accumulate enough to trigger the sensor."
safeChecks:
  - "Turn off power to the dishwasher at the breaker before removing the lower kick plate."
  - "Look for standing water in the base pan once the kick plate is off."
  - "Check that you're using dishwasher-specific detergent, not dish soap, which foams excessively."
  - "Inspect visible hose connections at the front of the unit for drips or looseness."
steps:
  - title: "Cut power and access the base pan"
    description: "Turn off the breaker, then remove the toe-kick panel at the bottom front of the dishwasher (usually just a couple of screws or clips)."
  - title: "Soak up any standing water"
    description: "Use a towel or small wet/dry vacuum to remove all water from the base pan so the float switch can reset."
  - title: "Check for an active drip"
    description: "With the panel still off, run a short cycle and watch closely for a few minutes to see if water is actively dripping from a hose or fitting."
  - title: "Tighten or address the source"
    description: "If you find a loose connection, tighten it by hand — avoid overtightening plastic fittings. If detergent foam is the cause, switch to a low-foam dishwasher detergent."
  - title: "Reset and test"
    description: "Reassemble the panel, restore power, and run a normal cycle to confirm the error doesn't return."
dontDo:
  - "Don't ignore the E15 code and keep restarting the cycle — this can allow a real leak to continue unnoticed."
  - "Don't use regular dish soap in a dishwasher — the foam it produces can trigger this exact error and cause overflow."
  - "Don't overtighten plastic hose fittings when checking connections — this can crack them and create a new leak."

faqs:

  - q: "Is E15 the same as a flood-safety shutoff?"
    a: "Yes — it's specifically Bosch's Aquastop system, designed to stop the unit before a leak becomes a flood."
  - q: "What detergent should I use to avoid this?"
    a: "Use a dishwasher tablet or powder formulated for machine use, and measure it correctly — too much of even the right detergent can cause excess foam."
