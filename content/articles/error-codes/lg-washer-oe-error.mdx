quickAnswer: "OE means the washer detected it couldn't drain within its expected time window — functionally the same issue as a washer not draining, just caught and reported as a specific code. A clogged pump filter is the most common cause, followed by a kinked drain hose."
symptoms:
  - "'OE' or 'OE1' shown on the display, usually appearing during or after the drain stage"
  - "Standing water remains in the drum when the error appears"
  - "The cycle pauses and won't proceed to spin"
  - "Error may repeat even after restarting the cycle"
causes:
  - title: "Clogged pump filter"
    description: "Lint, coins, and small items collect in the drain pump filter over time, restricting water flow enough to trip the drain-time-exceeded error."
  - title: "Kinked or crushed drain hose"
    description: "A drain hose bent sharply — often after the machine is moved — can restrict flow enough to trigger this code even with a clean filter."
  - title: "Blocked standpipe or household drain"
    description: "If the filter and hose are clear, the issue may be further downstream in the household drain line the washer empties into."
  - title: "Failed drain pump"
    description: "If everything upstream is clear, the pump motor itself may have failed and can no longer move water regardless of blockage."
safeChecks:
  - "Unplug the machine before opening the pump filter access panel, and have towels ready."
  - "Check the drain hose at the back of the machine for kinks, especially if it was recently moved."
  - "Confirm the household drain isn't backed up elsewhere (check a nearby sink for slow draining)."
steps:
  - title: "Cut power and access the filter"
    description: "Unplug the washer and open the small filter access panel near the bottom front, with towels laid down to catch residual water."
  - title: "Clean the pump filter"
    description: "Turn the filter cap slowly to drain remaining water, then remove it fully and clear out lint, coins, or debris."
  - title: "Check the drain hose"
    description: "Pull the machine out slightly and inspect the full length of the drain hose for kinks or tight bends."
  - title: "Reset and test"
    description: "Reassemble the filter, restore power, and run a drain or spin cycle to confirm the error clears and water flows normally."
dontDo:
  - "Don't skip unplugging the machine before opening the filter panel — water will spill and you don't want the machine powered during this."
  - "Don't force a stuck filter cap — if it won't turn, check your manual for the correct release method rather than forcing it."
  - "Don't run repeated cycles on the same error hoping it clears on its own."
whenToCallPro:
  - "The filter and hose are confirmed clear but the error persists."
  - "You hear no pump sound at all during the drain stage, suggesting the pump itself has failed."
  - "Water backs up into a nearby sink or floor drain when the washer tries to drain, suggesting a household plumbing blockage rather than a machine issue."
faqs:

  - q: "How often should I clean the pump filter to avoid this?"
    a: "Every 1–3 months for average use is a reasonable baseline, more often if you often wash items that shed fibers, pet bedding, or anything with loose small parts."
  - q: "Can this error damage the machine if ignored?"
    a: "Running the machine repeatedly with a blocked drain path can strain the pump motor over time, so it's worth resolving rather than repeatedly restarting the cycle."
