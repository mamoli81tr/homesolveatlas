  - title: "Failed door switch"

The distinction that matters most: does the door latch with a firm, single click, or does it take multiple attempts?

| What you notice | Likely direction | Safe next check |
|---|---|---|
| Something visibly caught in the door frame or seal | Physical obstruction | Clear it and reclose — the fastest fix and the most common cause |
| Door looks clear but the click feels soft or inconsistent | Lint buildup around the latch/switch | Clean the strike area and switch with a soft brush |
| Door only latches after several attempts, worsening over time | Misaligned latch | Check the door sits flush all the way around, not gapped on one side |
| Error appears with no pattern, latch feels and sounds normal | Failing door switch | Treat as a part replacement rather than more cleaning |
