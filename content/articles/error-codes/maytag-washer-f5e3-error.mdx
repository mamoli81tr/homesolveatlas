quickAnswer: "F5 E3 means the washer's door lock mechanism failed to confirm a secure lock. It's most often an obstruction in the door strike, a worn latch, or debris buildup preventing a full seal — all checkable without a technician."
symptoms:
  - "'F5 E3' shown on the display, usually right after pressing start"
  - "The door won't lock even when closed firmly"
  - "The cycle starts, then stops shortly after with the error showing"
  - "The door lock indicator flashes rather than staying solid"
causes:
  - title: "Obstruction in the door strike area"
    description: "A stray sock, small item, or detergent residue buildup in the door strike can prevent the latch from fully seating, so the lock switch never confirms a secure close."
  - title: "Worn or misaligned door latch"
    description: "Repeated use over years can wear the latch mechanism so it no longer reliably engages the lock switch."
  - title: "Detergent or fabric softener buildup on the door seal"
    description: "Residue on the rubber door gasket can prevent it from compressing fully, which in turn can prevent the latch from seating correctly."
  - title: "Failed door lock switch or motor"
    description: "The motorized lock assembly itself can fail electrically after years of use, which typically needs professional replacement."
safeChecks:
  - "Check the door strike and surrounding gasket for any obstruction or buildup, and clean with a damp cloth."
  - "Close the door firmly and listen for a distinct click from the lock mechanism."
  - "Power-cycle the machine (unplug for a minute) to clear a possible transient sensor confusion."
  - "Check that the door itself sits flush and isn't sagging or misaligned in its frame."
steps:
  - title: "Clean the door strike and gasket"
    description: "Wipe down the strike area and the full rubber door seal to remove detergent residue or debris that could be preventing a full seal."
  - title: "Power-cycle the washer"
    description: "Unplug it (or switch off the breaker) for a full 60 seconds, then restore power and try starting a cycle again."
  - title: "Test the latch"
    description: "Close the door several times, listening and feeling for a firm, consistent click each time rather than an inconsistent or soft catch."
  - title: "Check door alignment"
    description: "If the error persists, examine whether the door sits flush all the way around its frame rather than gapped on one side, which can indicate a hinge issue."
dontDo:
  - "Don't force the door shut repeatedly if it isn't latching normally — this can damage the latch mechanism further."
  - "Don't attempt to manually override or bypass the door lock switch to start a cycle — it's a safety interlock that prevents the drum from spinning with the door open."
  - "Don't ignore a flashing lock indicator hoping it resolves on its own."

faqs:

  - q: "Can I run the washer with the door not fully locking?"
    a: "No — the door lock is a safety feature that prevents the drum from spinning with the door open, so the machine won't proceed with a cycle without it."
  - q: "Does detergent residue really cause a door lock fault?"
    a: "Yes — buildup on the gasket or in the strike area is one of the most common and easiest-to-fix causes, since it physically prevents the door from seating fully even when it looks closed."
