One distinction worth making before you strip-wash anything: does the smell show up on towels straight from the linen closet, before they've even been used, or only on towels that have already been used and are waiting for their next wash? A musty smell on stored, unused towels points toward how they were dried and stored — folded away slightly damp, or kept in a humid closet — rather than anything happening in the wash cycle itself, and strip-washing alone won't fix a storage problem. A smell that only shows up on towels that have been used and are sitting in the hamper points more toward the wash cycle and machine causes below, since that's specifically where bacteria get the chance to establish themselves before the next wash.
